## 🌍 2. FREE PERMANENT CLOUD HOSTING (WORLDWIDE LIVE URL)

To get a permanent public link (e.g. `https://manfit-crm.netlify.app` or `https://manfit.store`) that anyone can open anywhere in the world on cellular 4G/5G data:

### Option A: Netlify Drop (Easiest — 10 Seconds, No Coding, Free Forever)
1. Open [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) in your browser.
2. Sign up or log in (free).
3. Drag and drop the **`MANFIT CRM`** folder directly into the designated drop area on the page.
4. **Done!** Netlify instantly provides a free, live HTTPS URL (e.g., `https://manfit-crm.netlify.app`).

---

### Option B: Vercel (1-Click Deployment, Free Forever)
1. Open [https://vercel.com/new](https://vercel.com/new).
2. Sign in with your GitHub or email.
3. Import the project or upload the folder.
4. The included `vercel.json` file automatically deploys the entire CRM with global CDN caching.

---

### Option C: GitHub Pages (Free Forever)
1. Create a new repository on [GitHub.com](https://github.com) named `manfit-crm`.
2. Upload the project files (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, etc.).
3. Go to **Settings** > **Pages**.
4. Under **Branch**, select `main` (root) and click **Save**.
5. Your site is live at:  
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/manfit-crm/`

---

## 📱 Mobile App (PWA) Feature
When you open your live URL on a smartphone (iPhone or Android):
- **iOS Safari**: Tap the **Share** button > **"Add to Home Screen"**.
- **Android Chrome**: Tap the **Three Dots (⋮)** > **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
- The CRM will now run as a full-screen, branded app on your mobile device!
